
# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
## Retail Stockout Analysis – DMart

### Business Problem
Pune has the lowest average daily revenue among
the five stores. The objective was to investigate
whether stockouts explain this performance.

### Key Findings
Pune generates an average daily revenue of
Rs. 636358.55 and records 85 poor-performance
days out of 90.

However, Pune ranks fourth in average stockout
hours at 2.32 hours. Hyderabad has the highest
stockout duration at 3.85 hours.

Pune's revenue has a stronger positive correlation
with footfall (0.735) than its negative correlation
with stockout hours (-0.254).

### Recommendation
Do not approve a stock-availability investment
programme based solely on the current evidence.

### Action Plan

1. Owner: Pune Store Manager
   Action: Investigate customer footfall and
   local marketing performance.
   Timeline: First 2 weeks.

2. Owner: Regional Operations Director
   Action: Review transaction-level sales,
   basket size and product mix.
   Timeline: Within 30 days.

3. Owner: Inventory Manager
   Action: Monitor stockout patterns and
   product availability.
   Timeline: Weekly for 30 days.

### Final Conclusion
Current evidence does not establish stockouts
as the primary cause of Pune's lower revenue.
Further investigation is recommended before
making major investment decisions.
