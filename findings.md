
# RETAIL STOCKOUT ANALYSIS – FINDINGS REPORT

## 1. Business Problem

Kavita Rao suspects that stockouts are responsible
for Pune's lower daily revenue.

This analysis investigates the relationship between
revenue, stockout hours and customer footfall.

## 2. Four Evidence-Based Facts

1. Pune has the lowest average daily revenue at
   Rs. 636358.55.

2. Pune recorded 85 poor-performance days out of 90.

3. Hyderabad has the highest average stockout hours
   at 3.85, while Pune ranks fourth at 2.32.

4. Pune's revenue-footfall correlation is 0.735,
   compared with -0.254 for revenue-stockout hours.

## 3. Business Insights

### Insight 1
Pune's average daily footfall is approximately 1518,
compared with Mumbai's 2391.

Customer traffic deserves further investigation.

### Insight 2
Stockouts alone cannot explain Pune's low revenue.
Hyderabad experiences higher stockout hours but
generates higher average daily revenue.

## 4. Unconfirmed Hypotheses

### Hypothesis 1
Lower customer traffic in Pune may be associated
with local demand, competition or marketing factors.

Additional data required:
Competitor activity, campaign performance and
customer traffic information.

### Hypothesis 2
Lower revenue per footfall may be associated with
basket size or product mix.

Additional data required:
Transaction-level sales, basket value and
product-category sales.

## 5. Correlation Analysis

| Analysis | Before Cleaning | After Cleaning |
|---|---:|---:|
| All stores: Revenue vs Stockout | -0.156 | -0.152 |
| All stores: Revenue vs Footfall | 0.548 | 0.918 |
| Pune: Revenue vs Stockout | -0.254 | -0.254 |
| Pune: Revenue vs Footfall | 0.744 | 0.735 |

## 6. Final Conclusion

The analysis does not support the claim that Pune
has the highest stockout problem.

Pune ranks fourth among five stores for average
stockout hours.

Revenue has a stronger positive relationship with
footfall than its negative relationship with stockouts.

Correlation does not establish causation.
Further investigation is required before approving
a stock-availability investment programme.
